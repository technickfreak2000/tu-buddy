Set up automatic TU login

Save your TUB account here once. TU Buddy will enter your credentials and generate one-time codes when you sign in.

Use the secret from your authenticator setup or the full otpauth://totp/… URI from its QR code. The six-digit code changes; the setup secret generates future codes. Leave empty to enter OTPs manually.

This is TU Buddy's setup page, provided by your extension. Your password and TOTP secret are stored locally, unencrypted, in this Firefox profile. Saving both here lets anyone with access to the profile sign in as you. The setup secret generates codes locally and is never entered into TU's webpage.
