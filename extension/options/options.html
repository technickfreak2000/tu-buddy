One-time passwords Optional

For unattended two-factor login, paste the setup secret from your authenticator setup, or the full otpauth://totp/… URI encoded in its QR code. A current six-digit code cannot generate future codes. TAN lists, push approvals and hardware keys still require you.

Keep this Firefox profile private. Your password and TOTP secret are saved locally, unencrypted, so login can work after restarting Firefox. They are never synced by this extension. Saving both factors together lets anyone with access to this profile sign in as you. Credentials are filled only on the exact TU Shibboleth HTTPS host. No analytics or third-party servers.

Independent extension, not affiliated with TU Berlin. TU sets session lifetimes; this extension signs you back in while you browse. Errors pause automation. Logging out pauses it until you choose Resume in the toolbar.
